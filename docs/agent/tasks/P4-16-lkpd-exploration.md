## Goal

Explore LKPD forms that change what students do, not only how the paper looks, for every SD/MI grade and every format (print, no device, digital interactive, print + digital, new technology with third-party AR/VR embeds). Back the ideas with tested 2025–2026 Indonesian SD studies, check them against what SIGRAK already decided, and analyse the risks of SIGRAK as a starter-LKPD maker that teachers and schools then adapt.
