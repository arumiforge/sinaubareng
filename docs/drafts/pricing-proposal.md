## Purpose

A first price list for the four tiers in [`product-concept.md`](../final/product-concept.md#business-model), with the reasons, so the owner can decide or adjust. Prices are per academic year (July–June), in rupiah, paid from non-BOS funds (D-025) and confirmed manually (D-091).

## What shapes the price

- **AI costs SIGRAK nothing.** Teachers use their own free Gemini keys (D-012, D-023), so the cost per user is small: the VPS, the domain, mail, and off-site backups are shared fixed costs. Prices can follow the value to the buyer, not a cost per user.
- **Who pays, and from what.** Teachers pay from their own pocket. Schools pay from committee, foundation, or other non-BOS funds, and the principal must justify it. Supervisors or KKG clusters pay from their own funds.
- **TKA rule.** The ministry pays for TKA and its preparation, and schools may not charge students or parents for TKA preparation. *Simulasi TKA* is therefore sold only inside Sigrak Sekolah, paid by the school, and sales material must never suggest passing the cost to parents.

## Market reference

| Product type | Examples (vendor pages) | Price |
|---|---|---|
| AI lesson-plan tools for one teacher | Prompt Guru, SIAP GURU | Rp79.000–125.000 per year |
| AI lesson-plan tools, monthly | Asisten Guru AI, Modulajar.app | Rp29.000–99.000 per month (Rp348.000–1.188.000 per year) |
| AI tool, school plan | Modulajar.app | Rp79.000 per teacher per month, minimum 6 teachers |
| School administration (SD/MI) | APPSO, Lentra, SANDIK, AdminSekolah.net | Rp650.000–2.500.000 per school per year; or Rp1.000–2.000 per student per month |
| School CBT | APPSO, Sekolahkita.net, Kohesi | Rp4.000.000–6.000.000 per school per year; Rp15.000–20.000 per student; hosting from Rp100.000 per year |
| Official TKA simulation | Pusmendik | Free |

SIGRAK sits between these groups. One teacher gets more than an AI lesson-plan tool (Di Kelas, Bank Soal, LKPD, Quiz Battle, print-ready exports), but brings their own AI key. A school gets teacher tools for every teacher, the portals, and Simulasi TKA in one subscription.

## Proposed prices

| Tier | Price per academic year | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Sigrak Dasar** | Free | Unchanged: monthly template limit, small watermark, no TKA sessions |
| **Sigrak Guru** | **Rp149.000** (about Rp12.500 per month) | Above the cheapest AI-only tools, because it adds administration and more templates. Far below monthly AI tools, because the teacher brings the AI key. Under the Rp150.000 mark |
| **Sigrak Sekolah**, small (up to 150 students) | **Rp1.500.000** | Same as an entry SD/MI administration package. Ten teachers buying Sigrak Guru alone would pay Rp1.490.000 |
| **Sigrak Sekolah**, medium (151–400 students) | **Rp2.500.000** | Matches the upper school-administration packages, and stays well below CBT-only products (Rp4–6 million). About Rp6.000–16.000 per student per year |
| **Sigrak Sekolah**, large (over 400 students) | **Rp3.500.000** | Same step up; still below CBT-only products |
| **Sigrak Binaan** | **Rp500.000** per supervisor or KKG cluster, **free** when 5 or more of its schools subscribe to Sigrak Sekolah | The supervisor portal is mainly a sales channel. Supervised schools get **15% off** Sigrak Sekolah |

Size is counted from the school's active students at the start of the academic year. MI and SD pay the same.

## Rules around the prices

1. **Annual, academic year.** A subscription bought from January to June costs half and ends on 30 June.
2. **Teacher to school.** When a teacher's school subscribes, the teacher's remaining Sigrak Guru time becomes a discount for the school, as already described in the product concept.
3. **Introductory price for 2026/2027 (optional).** Sigrak Guru Rp99.000, and 30% off Sigrak Sekolah for the first 50 schools. It helps the pilot and gives early testimonials; drop it in 2027/2028.
4. **Free trial for schools.** One month of Sigrak Sekolah for a school that a supervisor or the sales team brings in, using Mode Contoh first (D-090).
5. **Prices include VAT if SIGRAK must charge it**; check with an accountant before publishing.

## Before deciding

- Show the table to 5–10 principals and a few teachers during the short-version presentation (an open question in `product-concept.md`), and note who would pay and from which fund.
- Confirm SIGRAK's yearly fixed costs (VPS, domain, mail, R2, Healthchecks.io) so the small-school price still covers them at the expected number of schools.
- Recheck the market table before publishing: every figure comes from vendor pages on one day.
